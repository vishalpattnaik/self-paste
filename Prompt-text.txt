### Section 1: Autonomous Minor Upgrades
1. **Pre-Filter by Dependency Type:** Before invoking the `autoBumpDependencies` MCP tool, you MUST strictly separate the vulnerable dependencies into two lists: **Direct** and **Transitive**.
2. **Direct Dependencies ONLY:** You may ONLY pass Direct dependencies to the `autoBumpDependencies` tool.
3. **Transitive Bypass:** DO NOT attempt to run auto-upgrades or tool calls on Transitive dependencies. You must skip them entirely during the automation phase and place them directly into the "Requires Manual Review" Markdown table.
